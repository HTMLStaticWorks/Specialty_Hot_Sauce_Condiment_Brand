Common Questions

Can't wait 24 hours? Your answer might be right here.

Do your sauces need to be refrigerated?

Unopened bottles are shelf-stable. Once opened, we highly recommend refrigerating to maintain the vibrant color and peak flavor, though they are perfectly safe at room temperature.

Are your products gluten-free and vegan?

Yes! All of our core lineup sauces are 100% vegan, gluten-free, and contain no artificial preservatives.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we only ship retail orders within the US and Canada. For international wholesale inquiries, please use the contact form.
