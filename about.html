OUR STORY

Chasing The Perfect Burn.

Fyrza started with a simple belief: extreme heat shouldn't come at the cost of incredible flavor. What began as an obsession with fermenting backyard peppers has evolved into a relentless pursuit of culinary perfection. We don't make novelty sauces; we craft flavor enhancers that happen to pack a punch.

The Anti-Extract Movement.

Too many sauces rely on chemical extracts to achieve high Scoville ratings, resulting in a bitter, metallic taste that ruins your food. We reject that approach entirely.

By naturally fermenting our pepper mash and pairing it with whole fruits and toasted spices, we build complex heat profiles that bloom on the palate and elevate every bite.

Our Ingredient Philosophy

If you can't pronounce it, we don't use it. Nature provides all the flavor and fire we need.

Real Peppers Only

Absolutely no capsaicin extracts. Our heat comes purely from the peppers themselves.

Real Fruit

We use whole mangos, pineapples, and fresh citrus—never artificial fruit flavorings or concentrates.

Natural Spices

Toasted cumin, smoked paprika, and black garlic. Sourced globally, toasted locally.

Slow Fermentation

Time is an ingredient. We age our pepper mash to develop complex, funky, umami flavors.

The Making of Fyrza

01

Seed to Soil

We partner with ethical growers in Texas and New Mexico who understand that soil health directly dictates pepper flavor.

02

The Long Wait

The harvest is immediately crushed into mash and sealed in barrels. We let lacto-fermentation do the heavy lifting for 30+ days.

03

Bottling the Fire

The mash is blended, balanced, and bottled. Every single batch is taste-tested by our founders before a label goes on.

"We don't make hot sauce for people who want to prove how tough they are. We make hot sauce for people who want their food to taste incredible."
