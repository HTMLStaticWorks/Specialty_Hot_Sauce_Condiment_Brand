From Pepper to Bottle

We don't cut corners. Discover the meticulous process that gives Fyrza its unmistakable flavor.

1. The Harvest

We source our peppers directly from small family farms ensuring peak ripeness and boldest natural flavor.

2. Slow Fermentation

The pepper mash is aged in barrels for up to 30 days, allowing complex flavors to develop naturally.

3. The Blend

We blend the aged mash with fresh herbs, vinegars, and spices to create our signature flavor profiles.

4. Hand Bottled

Every batch is tested, bottled, and labeled with care, ready to elevate your next meal.
